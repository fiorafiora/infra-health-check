This check records what you saw at that moment. Nothing alerts you between monthly visits, and it is not 24-hour monitoring.

How the light is decided
  • Red: a checklist item or device is Abnormal, or something is Expired.
  • Amber: no red condition, but a follow-up is Open or In progress, or something is due within 30 or 90 days.
  • Green: the checklist has been filled in, and none of the red or amber conditions apply.
  • Not started: the checklist is still empty, and there is no abnormal item, open follow-up, or expiry warning. A Normal expiry on its own does not turn the light green.
  • If an issue comes back, keep the same ID and the original date first found.
  • Keep screenshots when something is abnormal. jpg, png, or webp, up to 5 MB each, 8 per item.
